Sri Sathya Sai Baba

Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba taught a simple message: Love All, Serve All. We try to live that message in our homes and centers.

USA Region 7

Our centers work together in devotion, service, and education across Northern California and Nevada. We host regional retreats, bhajans, and seva so centers can grow as one family.

Sai Organization

The Sri Sathya Sai International Organization works in the USA through regions and centers. Region 7 is one of those regions. We follow SSIO guidelines and share resources with centers.
