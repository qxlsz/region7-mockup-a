During month of May through June 2020, Region 7 SSE wing had organized Sai Parenting Workshops & Presentations as part of Unity is Divinity Series. You can access all the sessions from Educational & Sai Parenting Series - 2020.

Women's Mini Retreat

Once a year our Region has been hosting Women’s Mini Retreat where women from all centers get together and hear and participate in engaging and inspiring spiritual activities just for women.

Read more

Unlike previous years, in 2016, we will be exploring a new format to spiritually delve deeper in three successive ways. Each part will focus on one aspect of spiritual growth (Inspiration, Introspection, and Influence), and our plan is to have one event for each of these three areas, for a three-part WMR series in total. It is our hope that the new format will also enhance our women’s empowerment and continue to build a unique and nurturing place within our region for our sisters.
